## Seven-day content sequence

### Day 1 — Problem-led post
**Hook:** Local SEO client work gets repetitive when every audit starts from a blank sheet.

**Copy:**  
If you manage Google Business Profiles for clients, the work is not only about rankings. You also need to audit profile accuracy, map search intent to pages, plan useful content, respond to reviews and explain what changed each month.

I put those repeatable tasks into one editable toolkit: audit checklist, keyword map, review-response starters, 30-day post calendar, onboarding sheet, landing-page QA and reporting worksheet.

It is a working set of templates, not a ranking guarantee. See the contents and decide whether it fits your workflow.

**CTA:** View the Local SEO Agency Toolkit: https://digitalproductsbundle.in/products/local-seo-agency-toolkit

### Day 2 — Product walkthrough
Record a 30–60 second screen capture:
1. Open the GBP audit sheet.
2. Show how evidence, priority, owner and due date are recorded.
3. Open the keyword map and show how a keyword is tied to a target page.
4. Open the monthly report and show how visibility is separated from leads.
5. End on the product page.

Caption: “A quick look at the actual files inside the Local SEO Agency Toolkit. Editable CSV templates that open in Excel and Google Sheets. See the contents before buying.”

### Day 3 — Educational checklist
**Hook:** Five checks I would make before changing a Google Business Profile.

- Confirm the business name reflects the real-world name.
- Verify primary category and genuinely offered services.
- Test the phone and website link.
- Confirm address or service area reflects actual operations.
- Check that lead actions can be measured.

Explain that the full checklist is more detailed and the free post is useful on its own. Link to the toolkit naturally.

### Day 4 — Reporting education
**Hook:** Profile views are not the same as leads.

Explain the difference between impressions, clicks, calls, qualified enquiries and sales. Show how a monthly report can record both performance signals and the next action. Avoid implying that every metric is available for every profile.

CTA: View the reporting worksheet included in the toolkit.

### Day 5 — Common mistakes
Explain why agencies should avoid:
- Keyword-stuffing business names.
- Publishing near-duplicate location pages with little unique value.
- Copy-pasting identical review replies.
- Reporting impressions as revenue.
- Promising guaranteed rankings.

Show how a checklist helps standardize the process without replacing judgement.

### Day 6 — FAQ and fit
Answer:
- Who is this for?
- What files are included?
- Can I use it for client work?
- Does it include Google or third-party software?
- Does it guarantee rankings?
- How is the download delivered?

Be clear that it is an original template pack, not an official Google product.

### Day 7 — Product walkthrough and direct offer
Show the real workbook and the included sheets. State the price and one-time payment terms accurately. Invite questions and link to the product page. Do not create an artificial deadline or scarcity.

## Decision rules after the first 7–14 days

- If there are no qualified visits, improve distribution and audience targeting.
- If people visit but do not engage with the product page, improve relevance and previews.
- If people inspect the page but do not start checkout, investigate product fit, contents, trust and price.
- If checkout starts but no payments complete, inspect payment options, payment errors and friction.
- If a buyer pays but cannot download the files, pause promotion until delivery is fixed.
- If buyers use the templates and ask for the same additional feature, consider a version 1.1 based on repeated feedback.
